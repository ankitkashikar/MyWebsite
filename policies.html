Need help with an order?

The Customer Support Manager handles complaints. We review complaints daily and investigate the issue using the available order details and evidence.

Call +91 8956150583 or email chinesebliss1@gmail.com for order support, complaints or privacy requests.

Please share your order number, the mobile number used for the order and a short description of the issue. If an error shows a support reference, include it. Never share your UPI PIN, passwords or card security details.

To see your order’s progress, visit Order Status with your order number and mobile number.

For orders placed through Swiggy or Zomato, use the support options in the app where you placed the order.
