our-story

Two roommates, one shared craving, and a kitchen that started it all.

Ankit
From Jabalpur

[Placeholder] Ankit grew up in Jabalpur before moving to Pune, where a shared love of good food — and a serious craving for Indo-Chinese done right — set everything in motion.

Atul
From Nagpur

[Placeholder] Atul made his way from Nagpur to Pune, bringing an eye for flavour and a stubborn refusal to settle for average Chinese food. The rest, as they say, is history.

Chapter One

Two Roommates, One Kitchen

[Placeholder] Ankit and Atul weren't always business partners — they were just two roommates sharing a flat in Pune, trying to survive on takeout that never quite hit the spot. Late nights often ended the same way: standing in a cramped kitchen, arguing over how much chilli was too much chilli, and cooking up Indo-Chinese food that reminded them both of home.

Chapter Two

The Idea That Wouldn't Let Go

[Placeholder] What started as feeding themselves slowly turned into feeding friends, then neighbours, then anyone who happened to be around when the wok came out. The response was always the same: "you two should sell this." Eventually, they stopped ignoring it.

Chapter Three

The Chinese Bliss Is Born

[Placeholder] With a shared kitchen, a handful of recipes perfected over countless late nights, and zero background in running a restaurant, Ankit and Atul launched The Chinese Bliss — a cloud kitchen built on one simple promise: authentic Indo-Chinese, made fresh, every single time.

Today

Still Cooking, Still Roommates at Heart

[Placeholder] Today, The Chinese Bliss delivers across the city — through Swiggy, Zomato, and now our own kitchen door. The setup has grown, but the standard hasn't changed: if Ankit and Atul wouldn't eat it themselves, it doesn't leave the kitchen.
