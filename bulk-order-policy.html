Bulk Order Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Bulk orders require additional preparation, coordination, and delivery planning. The terms below apply to confirmed bulk orders placed directly with The Chinese Bliss and may be supplemented by the written quote or order confirmation issued for a specific booking.

1. Advance notice

Please place bulk-order requests at least 24 hours before the requested delivery time (IST). Acceptance depends on menu availability, quantity, preparation capacity, requested delivery time, and delivery arrangements.

2. Booking confirmation and advance payment

A bulk order is treated as confirmed only after The Chinese Bliss accepts the final order details and receives the required 50% advance payment.

Until the advance is received, a requested date or time should not be treated as finally reserved unless we confirm otherwise in writing.

3. Balance payment before dispatch

The remaining 50% balance must be paid before dispatch. We do not plan to provide post-delivery credit for bulk orders. The order may be held from dispatch until the outstanding balance is received and verified.

4. Final menu, quantities, and instructions

The final menu, quantities, delivery date/time, address, contact person, packaging requirements, and any important preparation instructions should be confirmed before production begins. Late changes are subject to availability and may affect price, preparation time, or delivery arrangements.

5. Delivery area, timing and charges

Bulk-order delivery is not restricted to PIN 411057. Please provide the full delivery address so the kitchen can confirm serviceability and delivery arrangements. Acceptance depends on the destination, preparation capacity and delivery availability. We do not promise delivery to every location. The PIN 411057 restriction applies to normal website orders.

The standard 35-50 minute direct-delivery estimate for regular orders does not apply to bulk orders. The expected dispatch and delivery plan will be agreed for each bulk order based on quantity, preparation requirements, destination, and third-party delivery availability.

Any bulk delivery charge, multiple-vehicle requirement, special handling cost, or other agreed delivery cost should be included or confirmed before final payment.

6. Cancellation and changes

Customer-requested cancellation is allowed only before the kitchen accepts the bulk order. After acceptance, cancellation for change of mind is unavailable. Requests to change quantities or delivery details must be discussed with support and are subject to kitchen agreement. Item complaints and remedies required by applicable law remain available.

If the kitchen cannot fulfil the bulk order, we will provide a full refund of the amount collected for that order. Other refund requests are reviewed by an authorised administrator under the Cancellation & Refund Policy.

7. Customer contact at dispatch

Please ensure the nominated recipient or coordinator is reachable near the agreed dispatch and delivery time. Delays or additional delivery costs caused by an incorrect address, restricted access, recipient unavailability, or requested changes after dispatch may be chargeable where a third-party delivery partner imposes those costs.

8. Quality or order issues

If a bulk order has a genuine missing, incorrect, damaged, spillage, or quality issue, contact us as soon as reasonably possible with the order details and photos where relevant. We will review the issue case by case and provide an appropriate resolution.

9. Current bulk-order menu

The public bulk-order menu is still being finalised. A bulk-order request is not considered final until the actual items, quantities, prices, payment schedule, and delivery details are confirmed by The Chinese Bliss.

10. Contact

For bulk-order planning, contact us at +91-8956150583 or chinesebliss1@gmail.com.

Bulk orders can require different commercial terms depending on size and customisation. The written confirmation for a specific bulk order should be read together with this policy.
