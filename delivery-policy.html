Customer Policy

Direct Delivery Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

This policy applies to orders placed directly through The Chinese Bliss website. Orders placed through Swiggy, Zomato, or another external platform are also subject to that platform's delivery terms.

1. Current direct-delivery area

Direct website delivery is currently intended for serviceable addresses in PIN code 411057, around Hinjewadi Phase 1, Pune. Final serviceability can depend on the exact address and availability of a delivery partner at the time of the order.

2. Direct-order hours

Our current direct-delivery ordering window is 4:00 PM to 12:00 AM. The website may offer "Deliver Now" only while direct ordering is open and may offer scheduled slots when available.

3. Estimated delivery time

For standard direct orders, our normal target is approximately 35-50 minutes from order confirmation. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed delivery time.

Preparation load, number of items, peak demand, traffic, heavy rain, road conditions, building or security access, delivery-partner availability, distance, and other circumstances outside our reasonable control can increase the actual delivery time.

4. Delivery partners

We may use a third-party hyperlocal delivery provider selected for the order. The provider may vary depending on serviceability, pricing, availability, and operating conditions. Where available, we may share delivery status or a tracking link with you.

To fulfil the delivery, we may share the minimum information reasonably required by the delivery provider, such as your name, phone number, delivery address, and order-related delivery instructions.

5. Delivery charges

Eligible direct orders with a food subtotal of ₹799 or more receive free delivery within the supported direct-delivery area.

For eligible orders below ₹799, a delivery charge may apply. The charge can vary with distance, time, demand, and the third-party delivery quote. Any delivery charge payable by you should be shown or confirmed before final payment or final order confirmation. We will not intentionally add an undisclosed delivery charge after you have accepted the final payable amount.

6. Scheduled orders

If you choose a future delivery slot, the selected time is a requested delivery window rather than an absolute guarantee. We will use reasonable efforts to prepare and dispatch the order for that slot. If there is a material issue with the requested slot, we may contact you using the phone number supplied with the order.

7. Customer availability and access

Please provide a complete address, landmark where useful, and an active phone number. You are responsible for being reasonably reachable when the order is being delivered and for providing any building, gate, reception, or security instructions needed to complete delivery.

If delivery cannot be completed because the address is incorrect, the recipient is unavailable or repeatedly unreachable, access is refused, or required delivery instructions are missing, additional delivery-partner charges may arise and a refund may be limited as described in our Cancellation & Refund Policy.

8. Delays and customer-caused delay

We handle meaningful delays case by case. A goodwill coupon or other resolution may be offered depending on the circumstances, but delay compensation is not automatic.

A delay coupon or similar goodwill benefit will normally not apply where the delay is materially caused by severe weather, flooding, traffic restrictions, unusually high demand, third-party rider shortages, events outside our reasonable control, an incorrect address, a requested change after ordering, customer unresponsiveness, or a request to intentionally delay the rider or handover.

9. Contact

For a direct-order delivery issue, contact The Chinese Bliss as soon as possible at +91-8956150583 or chinesebliss1@gmail.com.

Delivery times and third-party delivery charges are estimates or quotes affected by live operating conditions. The checkout amount and order confirmation should always be reviewed before payment.
