Customer Policy

Direct Delivery Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This policy applies to orders placed directly through The Chinese Bliss website. Orders placed through Swiggy, Zomato, or another external platform are also subject to that platform's delivery terms.

1. Current direct-delivery area

Normal website orders are limited to serviceable addresses in PIN code 411057, around Hinjewadi Phase 1, Pune. Bulk-order delivery is not restricted to PIN 411057. Each destination is subject to kitchen acceptance, preparation capacity and delivery availability. Submitting a bulk request does not guarantee delivery to every location.

2. Direct-order hours

Our normal direct-delivery ordering window is Monday–Sunday, 4:00 PM to 12:00 AM, IST. The website may offer "Deliver Now" only while direct ordering is open and may offer scheduled slots when available.

3. Estimated delivery time

For standard direct orders, our normal target is approximately 35-50 minutes from order confirmation. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed delivery time unless we expressly state otherwise for a particular order.

Preparation load, number of items, peak demand, traffic, heavy rain, road conditions, building or security access, delivery-partner availability, distance, and other circumstances outside our reasonable control can increase the actual delivery time.

4. Delivery partners

We may use a third-party hyperlocal delivery provider selected for the order. The provider may vary depending on serviceability, pricing, availability, and operating conditions. You can check staff-updated order progress on our Order Status: Accepted → Preparing → Food is Ready → Dispatched. Dispatched means the order has left the kitchen. It does not confirm delivery. Live rider location and external delivery tracking are not provided.

To fulfil the delivery, we may share the minimum information reasonably required by the delivery provider, such as your name, phone number, delivery address, and order-related delivery instructions.

5. Delivery charges

Delivery charges and any free-delivery threshold are shown in checkout before payment. Rules can differ for normal and bulk orders. Free-delivery eligibility uses the food subtotal after discounts, excluding delivery charges.

If delivery is unavailable for your order type, checkout will stop. Review the final payable amount before payment. If pricing changes before submission, you must review checkout again.

6. Scheduled orders

Bulk requests must be placed at least 24 hours before the requested delivery time, calculated in IST. Bulk delivery timing is agreed separately. The normal 35–50 minute estimate does not apply. See the Bulk Order Policy.

If you choose a future delivery slot, the selected time is a requested delivery window rather than an absolute guarantee. We will use reasonable efforts to prepare and dispatch the order for that slot. If there is a material issue with the requested slot, we may contact you using the phone number supplied with the order.

7. Customer availability and access

Please provide a complete address, landmark where useful, and an active phone number. You are responsible for being reasonably reachable when the order is being delivered and for providing any building, gate, reception, or security instructions needed to complete delivery.

If delivery cannot be completed because the address is incorrect, the recipient is unavailable or repeatedly unreachable, access is refused, or required delivery instructions are missing, additional delivery-partner charges may arise and a refund may be limited as described in our Cancellation & Refund Policy, subject to applicable law.

8. Delays and customer-caused delay

We handle meaningful delays case by case and will communicate material timing changes where reasonably possible. Depending on the cause and impact, a resolution may include a refund where required by applicable law, replacement, account credit, a goodwill coupon, or another reasonable remedy.

A goodwill coupon or similar delay benefit will normally not apply where the delay is materially caused by severe weather, flooding, traffic restrictions, unusually high demand, third-party rider shortages, events outside our reasonable control, an incorrect address, a requested change after ordering, customer unresponsiveness, or a request to intentionally delay the rider or handover.

Nothing in this policy limits a consumer right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded. Where applicable law requires a refund or other remedy for late delivery that is not caused by force majeure or the customer, that right will be respected.

9. Contact

For a direct-order delivery issue, contact The Chinese Bliss as soon as possible at +91-8956150583 or chinesebliss1@gmail.com.

Delivery times are estimates affected by operating conditions. Review the delivery charge and final order total at checkout before payment. For delivery questions, contact support using the details above.
