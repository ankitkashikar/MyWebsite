Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

This policy explains how The Chinese Bliss handles information you provide when you browse our website, contact us, or place an order through our online ordering experience.

1. Information you may provide

Depending on how you use the website, you may provide information such as:

  • Your name and phone number.
  • Your delivery address and order details.
  • Your selected payment method and any order notes you choose to submit.
  • Information you send when contacting us for support or an order query.

2. How we use information

We use information submitted through the website to operate the ordering experience and communicate with you about your request. This can include processing an order, confirming order details, arranging delivery, responding to support questions, and maintaining the security and reliability of the website.

3. Payments

The website may offer payment options such as UPI or cash on delivery. The Chinese Bliss website does not ask you to enter your UPI PIN, card PIN, or online-banking password. Payment confirmation may occur separately from the initial order submission.

4. Service providers

We may use hosting, database, communications, payment, or delivery-related service providers where needed to operate the website and fulfil orders. Those providers may process information only as required to provide their services or meet legal obligations.

5. Technical information

When you visit the website, hosting and infrastructure providers may automatically receive basic technical information such as an IP address, browser or device information, and request logs. This information can be used for security, troubleshooting, and service reliability.

6. Data retention

We aim to keep personal information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for order fulfilment, customer support, record-keeping, security, or applicable legal requirements.

7. Your choices

If you want to ask about information you previously submitted, or request a correction or deletion where applicable, contact us at chinesebliss1@gmail.com.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information handled through the website. No internet service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive information that is not required for your order.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website, ordering process, or our practices change. The date at the top of this page shows the latest published version.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact The Chinese Bliss at chinesebliss1@gmail.com.

This page is a practical website policy for The Chinese Bliss and should be reviewed whenever the ordering, payment, delivery, or data-handling setup changes.
