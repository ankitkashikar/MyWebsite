Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This policy explains how The Chinese Bliss handles personal information when you browse our website, place or schedule a direct order, submit a bulk-order request, contact us, or interact with our ordering and delivery process.

1. Information you may provide

Depending on how you use the website, we may receive information such as:

  • Your name and mobile number.
  • Your delivery address, landmark, and delivery instructions.
  • Your selected delivery date or slot, order details, quantities, and special instructions.
  • Any allergy, spice, packaging, or other note you voluntarily include with an order.
  • Your selected payment method, payment reference or status where needed to verify a transaction, and coupon information.
  • Information you provide when contacting us about support, a complaint, refund, cancellation, or bulk order.

The Chinese Bliss website does not ask you to disclose your UPI PIN, card PIN, CVV, or online-banking password. Do not send those credentials to us.

2. How we use information

We use information provided through the website as reasonably necessary to:

  • accept, confirm, prepare, schedule, and fulfil orders.
  • arrange delivery and communicate delivery instructions.
  • verify payment status and investigate failed or duplicate payments.
  • contact you about availability, timing, substitutions, cancellations, refunds, or support.
  • manage coupons, fraud prevention, abuse prevention, and website security.
  • maintain order, accounting, complaint, and operational records as reasonably required.
  • provide authorised administrators with customer contact details, order-time addresses, website order history, order counts, frequently ordered items and common ordering times in IST for customer service and operational review.
  • improve the reliability and usability of the ordering experience.

3. Order and delivery notifications

Order progress is available on the Order Status using your order number and the mobile number used for that order. The website does not send automated WhatsApp, SMS or email order notifications. We may still use your contact details to clarify or support an order or arrange delivery.

The status page remembers your most recent order number and mobile number in your browser’s session storage to make repeat checks easier during that session. Avoid using a shared device for order lookup, or close the tab when finished.

4. Delivery partners

We may use third-party hyperlocal delivery providers for direct orders. The provider may vary based on serviceability and availability. To arrange delivery, we may share the minimum information reasonably needed by that provider, which can include your name, phone number, delivery address, landmark, and delivery instructions.

The delivery provider may process that information under its own privacy terms when providing the delivery service.

5. Payments

When an online payment provider is enabled, payment processing may be handled by that provider. Payment providers may receive transaction information needed to process and verify your payment.

Do not send us sensitive authentication credentials such as your UPI PIN, card PIN, CVV, or online-banking password. Where payment confirmation is initially manual, we may retain the payment reference or verification status reasonably needed to reconcile the order.

6. Technology and service providers

We may use service providers for website hosting, database storage, order processing, communications, payment processing, delivery fulfilment, security, and technical support. These providers may process relevant information only as needed to provide their services, comply with law, prevent misuse, or protect their systems.

The current website uses Supabase for order-related backend and database functionality. Other providers may be introduced as the payment, notification, and delivery integrations are finalised.

7. Technical information

Errors may include a support reference that helps us locate technical diagnostics. When reporting an error, provide that reference and a short description. Do not send passwords, payment credentials or unnecessary personal information.

When you access the website, hosting and infrastructure services may automatically process basic technical information such as IP address, browser or device information, timestamps, request logs, error logs, and security signals. This can be used for troubleshooting, fraud or abuse prevention, security, and service reliability.

8. Data retention

We retain order and customer records for fulfilment, customer support, payment reconciliation, accounting, complaint handling, security and dispute resolution. We have not yet set fixed retention periods for each category of record.

There is currently no automatic deletion schedule. You may contact us about your information using the details below. A request does not automatically erase records. It requires review of the relevant records and any applicable retention or other legal obligations.

9. Your requests and choices

You may contact us if you want to ask what information you previously submitted, request correction of inaccurate information, or request deletion or another privacy-related action where applicable. Some information may need to be retained where required for accounting, legal compliance, fraud prevention, an unresolved order, or a dispute.

Send privacy requests to chinesebliss1@gmail.com and provide enough information for us to identify the relevant order or interaction without sending unnecessary sensitive data.

10. Security

Order and customer records are held in our Supabase-backed systems, with access restricted to authorised operations and administration. Sensitive server credentials are not provided through the public website. No internet service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, so please avoid sending information that is not required for your order or support request.

11. External platforms and links

If you choose to order through Swiggy, Zomato, a delivery provider, payment provider, or another external service, that provider's own privacy policy and terms apply to its processing of your information. The Chinese Bliss does not control the privacy practices of independent external platforms.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, ordering process, payment setup, delivery partners, notifications, or legal obligations change. The date at the top of this page shows the latest published version.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact The Chinese Bliss Support using our existing contact details: chinesebliss1@gmail.com or +91-8956150583.

For a privacy question or request, use the support contact details above. Please provide only the information needed to identify your order or explain your request.
