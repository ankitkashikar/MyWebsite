Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Our food is generally prepared after an order is accepted, so cancellation and refund eligibility depends on how far the order has progressed. We aim to resolve genuine order problems fairly while also accounting for food already prepared and third-party delivery costs already incurred.

1. Cancelling a direct order

If you need to cancel or change an order, contact us immediately. A cancellation requested before preparation begins and before a delivery partner has been committed is more likely to be accepted.

Once preparation has started, cancellation may not be possible because the food is made to order and is perishable.

2. Delivery-partner charges after cancellation

If a third-party delivery partner has already been assigned, reached the pickup point, collected the order, or incurred a cancellation/reverse-delivery charge, that actual unavoidable delivery cost may be taken into account when deciding the refundable amount, subject to applicable law and the circumstances of the cancellation.

3. Cancellation by The Chinese Bliss

We may need to decline or cancel an order because an item is unavailable, the order cannot be prepared safely or within reasonable capacity, the delivery address is not serviceable, a payment cannot be verified, or another operational issue prevents fulfilment.

If we cancel a prepaid direct order for a reason attributable to us and the order cannot be fulfilled, the amount collected for the unfulfilled order will be refunded or otherwise resolved with you.

4. Wrong, missing, spilled, damaged, or quality-related orders

Please contact us as soon as reasonably possible if you receive the wrong item, an item is missing, packaging has materially failed, food has spilled in transit, or there is another genuine quality concern.

We may ask for photographs, the order number, item details, and other reasonable information needed to verify the issue. Depending on the facts, the resolution may include replacement, partial or full refund, account credit, or a goodwill coupon.

5. Delivery delays

Delivery times shown by us are estimates unless we expressly state a guaranteed delivery schedule. Significant delays are reviewed case by case. Depending on the cause and impact, the resolution may include a refund where required by applicable law, replacement, account credit, a goodwill coupon, or another reasonable remedy.

Goodwill delay benefits are not automatic where the delay is materially caused by severe weather, flooding, traffic restrictions, peak demand, third-party rider shortages, an incorrect or incomplete address, building or security access, customer unresponsiveness, a customer-requested order change, a request to delay the rider, or another circumstance outside our reasonable control.

Nothing in this policy limits a consumer right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded. Where applicable law requires a refund or other remedy for late delivery that is not caused by force majeure or the customer, that right will be respected.

6. Failed delivery because the customer is unavailable

If the order reaches the delivery location but delivery cannot be completed after reasonable contact attempts because the recipient is unavailable or unreachable, access is not provided, the address is materially incorrect, or the customer refuses delivery without a valid order issue, the food and delivery charges may be non-refundable because the order has already been prepared and the delivery service used, subject to applicable law.

7. Payment issues and duplicate charges

If an online payment is charged but the order is not accepted, or you believe a duplicate payment has occurred, contact us with the payment reference and order details. We will verify the transaction before initiating any applicable refund.

Approved refunds are normally returned through the original payment route where possible. The time for the amount to appear after initiation depends on the bank, UPI app, card network, or payment gateway.

8. Food returns

Because prepared food is perishable, we generally do not accept physical returns for change of mind, taste preference, or an order placed by mistake after preparation has started. This does not limit any remedy available under applicable law for defective, deficient, unsafe, incorrectly supplied, or unlawfully late food/services.

9. Bulk orders

Bulk orders have separate advance-payment and fulfilment terms. Please review the Bulk Order Policy and the specific written quote or confirmation for that order.

10. How to request a resolution

Contact us at +91-8956150583 or chinesebliss1@gmail.com. Please include your order number, phone number used for the order, a short description of the issue, and photos where relevant.

Each order issue is reviewed on its own facts. Nothing in this policy is intended to remove rights or remedies that a consumer has under applicable Indian law.
