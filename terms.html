Submitting an order through the website sends us an order request. An order is subject to item availability, preparation capacity, address serviceability, payment status, and confirmation. We aim to keep menu descriptions, prices, and availability accurate, but they may change. The final payable amount shown or confirmed for the order is the amount you should review before payment.

For eligible direct orders with a food subtotal of ₹799 or more, our current policy is free delivery within the supported direct-delivery area. Below ₹799, a third-party delivery charge may apply and can vary with distance, time, demand, and partner pricing. Please see the Delivery Policy.

4. Payments

Available payment methods are shown at checkout. Direct website orders may use UPI or another online payment method once enabled. Cash on Delivery is not currently planned for direct website orders unless it is expressly shown as available at checkout.

5. Delivery timing

The current direct-order window is 4:00 PM to 12:00 AM. For standard direct orders, our normal delivery target is approximately 35-50 minutes from order confirmation. This is an estimate, not a guarantee.

Preparation load, peak demand, traffic, heavy rain, road conditions, building access, distance, and delivery-partner availability can affect actual timing. See the Direct Delivery Policy for more detail.

6. Scheduled orders

Where scheduling is offered, the selected delivery slot is a requested window. We will use reasonable efforts to prepare and dispatch for that slot, but a scheduled time can still be affected by operational conditions. Please keep the phone number supplied with the order reachable in case we need to confirm a material timing change.
